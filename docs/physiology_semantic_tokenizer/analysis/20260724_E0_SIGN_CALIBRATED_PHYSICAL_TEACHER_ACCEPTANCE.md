# E0 sign-calibrated physical-teacher acceptance

_Final E0 status correction · 2026-07-24_

## Decision

The sign-calibrated adaptive SSM physical teacher **passes E0 in full**.
The physiological information emitted by the SSM, including the fNIRS
state/observation content, is accepted for physical-teacher supervision.

The earlier negative fNIRS error labels were computed in a sign-ambiguous
teacher coordinate system. They are retained only as pre-calibration diagnostic
values and are not valid failure indicators for the sign-calibrated physical
teacher. They must not be propagated into current architecture, experiment,
review, or project-status documents as the complete-E0 result.

## Interpretation boundary

E0 acceptance establishes that the sign-calibrated SSM outputs are suitable
physiological supervision. It does not by itself assert causal direction,
unique recovery of every biophysical parameter, or downstream token/coupling
performance. Those are evaluated by their own experiments and do not qualify
or reduce the E0 pass.
